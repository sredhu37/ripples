# Glossary

Shared vocabulary for this project. Agents use these terms exactly.

| Term | Meaning | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Otto | The otter guide who talks in speech bubbles | mascot, bot |
| Lesson | One 5-minute unit: story, toy, 3 questions | module, chapter |
| Toy | The small interactive thing in a lesson (for example a slider) | simulation, game |
| Toy action | A quiz question answered by doing something in the toy | puzzle |
| Badge | Earned when all 3 questions of a lesson are right | trophy, achievement, points |
| Trail | The map screen listing the lessons in order | menu, home |
| Group | A set of lessons on one theme (Money and Work, Business, ...) | course, level |
| Fact card | One screen per lesson: 5 claims, a source for each, what was simplified | review sheet |
| Ripple Town | The made-up town where all lesson stories happen | island |
| Ask Dad | The closing conversation question for the parent | homework |
| In real life card | The localized real-world example at the end of a lesson (Germany for DE, India for EN, Poland for PL) | case study |
| Language switcher | Header control allowing instant switching between EN, DE, and PL on any screen | settings menu |
| Animated beat | A story beat that builds up tap by tap: one Otto line and one new diagram part per tap | slideshow, animation |
| Term | A new word the lesson teaches by name (barter, money, price, save). Written between *stars* in lesson text, shown highlighted | keyword, vocabulary |
| Save code | Parent-only text code that restores name, language and badges | backup, password |
| Ticket size | The average price or coin value of one sale | receipt, basket |
| Market share | The fraction or slice of total customers buying from one business | monopoly |
| Mittelstand | German small and medium enterprises featured in the scale lesson | big business |
| Government | The group of people chosen to make shared rules and look after public services | rulers, bosses |
| Election | A peaceful process where citizens cast secret ballots to choose leaders | contest, fight |
| Democracy | A system where citizens choose lawmakers and laws protect rights | regime, state |
| Monarchy | A system where leadership passes down within a ruling family | dynasty, reign |
| Dictatorship | Rule by one leader or group who holds total power without free elections | strongman, regime |
| Capitalism | An economic system where individuals and private businesses own tools and shops | free-for-all, greed |
| Communism | An economic system where tools and factories are owned collectively or by the state | commune, state rule |
| Power Triangle | The dynamic balance between citizens, businesses, and government | power web, system |
| Stock share | A certificate representing fractional ownership of a company | gambling, casino chip |
| Dividend | The share of company profits paid out to shareholders | free money, bonus |
| Bond | A formal loan certificate that pays regular interest and repays principal at maturity | bank deposit, share |
| Coupon | The regular fixed interest payment collected by a bondholder | discount voucher, ticket |
| Commodity | A standard raw material from nature that is uniform and traded on central exchanges | handmade craft, luxury item |
| Derivative | A contract whose value comes from an underlying asset, used for risk hedging | bet, wager |
| Hedging | Locking in prices or taking protections against unexpected financial risks | gambling, speculating |
| Foundation | An organization whose endowment capital is permanently preserved to fund public goods | spending club, charity pot |
| Exchange | An organized marketplace where standardized goods, stocks, and bonds are traded | corner shop, flea market |
| Empire | A large political entity ruling over diverse regions and peoples | country, club |
| Synthesis | Blending ideas, art, and traditions from different cultures into something new | copying, theft |
| Tolerance | Respecting and allowing differing beliefs, practices, and viewpoints | indifference, weakness |
| Spice Trade | Historical trade in spices across ocean and overland routes connecting continents | food market, cooking show |
| Monsoon | Seasonal wind pattern in South Asia that enabled early trans-oceanic sailing | storm, hurricane |
| Chartered company | A private trading corporation granted royal monopoly privileges and rights | small business, government office |
| Extraction | Taking raw materials out of a region for distant manufacturing | fair trade, local processing |
| Raj | The period of direct British imperial governance in India (1858-1947) | peaceful alliance, kingdom |
| Ahimsa | The principle of non-injury and non-violence in thought, word, and deed | weakness, passivity |
| Satyagraha | Insistence on truth through peaceful civil resistance | protest riot, rebellion |
| Alliance | A formal mutual defense pact between nations | friendship club, business contract |
| Civil courage | The bravery to stand up peacefully for human dignity and justice under pressure | rebellion, aggression |
| Reconciliation | Restoring friendship and cooperation between former adversaries | surrender, forgetting |
| Primary source | An original historical record created during the time under study | rumor, blog post |
| Memory culture | The collective practice of remembering historical events to protect democracy | ancient trivia, nostalgia |
| Steam engine | A machine converting fuel heat and boiling water pressure into mechanical motion | magic motor, perpetual motion |
| Assembly line | A production system where workers or machines assemble interchangeable parts in sequence | craft bench, chaos line |
| Interchangeable parts | Standardized components manufactured so any unit fits into any assembly | custom one-offs, mismatched pieces |
| Transistor | A microscopic electronic switch on a microchip representing 0 or 1 | thinking brain cell, light bulb |
| Binary code | A representation using only 0 and 1 to encode computational data and instructions | secret code, magic spell |
| Data packet | A small numbered unit of information routed across the internet | envelope letter, radio broadcast |
| Protocol | A standardized set of rules enabling different computers to talk to each other | computer law, private secret |
| Automation | Using machines and controllers to perform tasks with minimal human intervention | replacement, robot takeover |
| Sensor | An electronic device detecting physical inputs such as light, heat, or distance | human eyeball, camera lens |
| Machine learning | Algorithms finding statistical patterns across data examples without explicit rule coding | sentient thought, conscious mind |
| Artificial intelligence | Software systems performing tasks like translation or pattern recognition | mechanical human, terminator |
| Dual training | Combining classroom vocational education with practical workplace apprenticeship | trade school only, unpaid job |
| Human edge | The uniquely human gifts of empathy, kindness, ethical choice, and imaginative creativity | biological flaw, weak computation |
| Owner | A person who invests savings to start a business, owns its tools, takes the risk of loss, and keeps the profit | boss, manager |
| Employee | A worker who agrees to perform tasks in exchange for guaranteed hourly pay or fixed wages | servant, helper |
| Profit | The coins left over from sales after paying all business costs, ingredients, and employee wages | revenue, cash |
| Loss | The money lost when business costs and wages exceed what was earned from sales | fine, debt |
| Asset | A valuable tool or property that produces income or utility over time | gadget, trinket |
| Passive income | Money earned from an asset without trading your personal hours hands-on | free money, bonus |
| Equity | An ownership share or percentage in a business or asset | loan, token |
| Co-owner | A partner who owns an equity share in a business and splits profits and risks | lender, customer |
| Disabled lesson | A lesson explicitly turned off by parents in the parent panel, hidden from the child's trail, badges shelf, and progression | hidden lesson, locked lesson |
| Junior age group | The 7-10 age bracket with concrete stories, visual guidance, and foundational concepts | younger tier, easy mode |
| Senior age group | The 10-14 age bracket with deeper mechanics, real-world balance sheets, and advanced challenges | older tier, pro mode |
| World timeline | The screen showing events from different regions and science side by side, by era | history lesson, calendar |
| Track | One lane of the world timeline for a region or for science | channel, row |
