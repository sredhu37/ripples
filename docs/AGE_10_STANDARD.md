# Senior Tier (Ages 10-14) Age-10 Target Readability Standard

## 1. Audience Target Baseline
- Target age: Typical 10-year-old child (Grade 5).
- Vocabulary level: Direct, concrete, everyday conversational English/German/Polish.
- Intellect: Smart, curious, capable of grasping sophisticated systems when anchored in physical objects.

## 2. Story Beat Guidelines
- **Max Sentence Length**: 15 words per sentence.
- **Physical Anchor Rule**: Before introducing any technical or economic concept, anchor it in an everyday physical object or relatable kid activity (e.g., pizza slices, sneaker drops, lemonade, gaming PC builds, dominoes, light switches).
- **Structure**: Exactly 5 animated beats per story, exactly 3 lines per beat.

## 3. Diagram / Visual Guidelines
- **Badge / Label Length**: Maximum 4 words per label or badge inside SVGs.
- **Visual Clarity**: High contrast, self-explanatory icons, and minimal reading burden. The diagram must relieve cognitive fatigue, not add to it.

## 4. Question & Quiz Guidelines
- Exactly 3 questions: 2 choice, 1 widget.
- **Question Prompt**: Clear, conversational, max 20 words.
- **Answer Options**: Under 15 words per option. Single-clause phrasing. No walls of text.
- **Distractors**: Plausible but distinct; fun and gentle tone.

## 5. Style & Constraints
- **Zero em dashes (`—` or `–`)**: Strictly forbidden everywhere in text, SVGs, prompts, hints, and source citations.
- **Trilingual Parity**: EN, DE, and PL versions must share the exact same simple structure and character names (Indian for EN, German for DE, Polish for PL — strictly no Bartek).
